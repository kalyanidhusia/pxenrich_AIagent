How this works

Public data only

Every study here is a public dataset from the ProteomeXchange network of repositories (PRIDE, MassIVE, iProX, jPOST), with links back to the original records and publications.

Labels carry receipts

When a study is labeled “triple-negative” or “patient-derived,” you can open the label and read the exact text it came from, where that text lives, and how confident the system is.

Honest uncertainty

Unknown, mixed, conflicting, and “deliberately not labeled” are real answers. A confident claim without a source is treated as a defect, not a feature.

pxEnrich explains research evidence. It does not diagnose disease, recommend treatment, or interpret personal medical records. For medical decisions, please talk with a clinician.
